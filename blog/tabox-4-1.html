Tabox 4.1 Is Here: Introducing Full-Page View

June 4, 2026

If you live in your browser, you know the pain: dozens of open tabs, half-remembered research sessions, and project links scattered everywhere. Tabox is the Chrome and Edge extension that fixes that, letting you save open tabs and tab groups into named, searchable collections and reopen them in a click.

Today we're shipping our biggest update yet: Tabox 4.1, headlined by the all-new Full-Page View.

New: Full-Page View

The popup is great for quick saves, but when you're really organizing, you want room to work. Full-Page View opens Tabox in a full browser tab, giving you a spacious workspace to manage everything at once:

  • A full-width header and dedicated sidebar for fast navigation between folders and collections.
  • Room to see far more collections and tabs on screen, with no more squinting through a tiny popup.
  • A redesigned, frosted-glass interface that's clean, modern, and comfortable to use for longer sessions.

It's the best way to reorganize your tab library, clean up old collections, or plan out a big project, all in one view.

Multi-Color Collection Filtering

Filter your collections by multiple colors at once. Tag collections by color, such as work, personal, research, or urgent, and combine them to surface exactly what you need. A new color picker with gradient preview and a one-tap Clear button makes slicing through large libraries effortless.

Smarter Tab Groups

  • Tab-count badges show how many tabs live in each saved group at a glance.
  • A new Open All Tabs button restores an entire collection in one click.
  • Chrome tab-group colors are now preserved correctly when collections are saved and reopened.

Accurate "Last Opened" Tracking

Tabox now records when you last opened a collection authoritatively in the background, so your most recently used collections always sort accurately, even across windows and sessions.

Better Drag and Drop

Reordering tabs is clearer than ever with a drop indicator that shows exactly where a tab will land as you drag.

Get Tabox 4.1

Tabox is free, privacy-respecting (your data stays local, with optional Google Drive sync), and works on Chrome 89+ and Edge. Update from the Chrome Web Store or install it fresh, then open Full-Page View and take control of your tabs today.
