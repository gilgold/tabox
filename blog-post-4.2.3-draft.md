Tabox AI already had a lot of useful tools. The problem was remembering which one to open. Tabox 4.2.3 fixes that with a single question.

## One box, all of Tabox AI

Open Tabox AI and it now asks: **"What would you like to do?"**

Type it in your own words — "clean up my messy collections", "find that recipe tab I saved last month", "group my open tabs" — and Tabox picks the right tool for you. No menus to learn.

Not sure where to start? Four suggestion chips get you going: **Plan something**, **Organize my tabs**, **Tidy my collections**, **Find a saved tab**.

And if your request isn't clear enough, Tabox asks a quick follow-up question instead of guessing. Nothing is ever changed without showing you a preview first — you're always the one who says yes.

## Task Planner: describe a project, get a collection

The newest AI tool is a chat. Tell it what you're working on — a trip to Lisbon, learning watercolor, comparing baby strollers — and it builds a ready-to-use collection of relevant pages for you.

- It suggests fresh starting ideas every time you open it
- It checks that every link actually works before adding it
- It can add to a collection you already have, not just start a new one
- When you're done, it offers to share the collection or drop it into a folder

## A nicer welcome

New to Tabox? The welcome guide got a full visual refresh, with the same soft frosted-glass look as Tabox AI, and animated previews of what each feature does. If you prefer less motion, it respects your system's reduced-motion setting.

## Small things that make a difference

- The dark mode switch now always matches what's actually on your screen
- The share-link window no longer hides behind the AI window

## Get it

Tabox 4.2.3 rolls out automatically through the Chrome Web Store, Microsoft Edge Add-ons, and Firefox Add-ons. Tabox AI is part of Tabox Pro, which you can upgrade to from inside the extension; every free feature stays free.
